# [F. Rectangle](./F-Rectangle.cpp)

You are given the length and breadth of a rectangle. Your task is to calculate its area and perimeter.

The formulas are:

- Area = length × breadth
- Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth)

## Input

A single line containing two integers `length` and `breadth` (`1 ≤ length, breadth ≤ 1000`).

## Output

Print the area and perimeter in the format shown in the example.

## Example

### Input

```text
5 7
```

### Output

```text
Area = 35
Perimeter = 24
```

# [H1. Calculator](./H1-Calculator.cpp)

You are given two integers `N` and `M`. Your task is to compute and print the results of the following operations:

- N + M
- N − M
- N × M
- N ÷ M (integer division)
- N mod M

## Input

A single line containing two integers `N` and `M` (`1 ≤ N, M ≤ 1000`).

## Output

Print five lines in the format shown in the example.

## Example

### Input

```text
6 4
```

### Output

```text
6 + 4 = 10
6 - 4 = 2
6 * 4 = 24
6 / 4 = 1
6 % 4 = 2
```
